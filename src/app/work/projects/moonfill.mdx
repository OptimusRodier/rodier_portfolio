## Overview

Moonfill is a browser extension built for Awin agents. Before, running a Moonpull integration scan meant filling in the Moonpull form, waiting for the result to arrive in Teams, copying it and pasting it into Salesforce.

Now an agent clicks one button on the program's record and Moonfill does all of it: it reads the program, **MID** and **country** from the record, submits the form, follows the scan and posts the result back in the record's Chatter feed.

---

## Why it is useful

- **One click.** No typing the MID or region, no form to fill in, no copy and paste.
- **Nothing to remember.** The program, MID and country come straight from the Salesforce record.
- **Time to do other things.** While a scan runs (usually up to 10 minutes) the agent can use other tabs and other apps.
- **Several scans at once.** Scans can run in parallel, for the same client or different ones, and each result goes to its own record.
- **Always visible.** One click on the extension icon shows what is running, what is done and what needs attention, with a history of the latest scans.

---

## How it works

1. On the program's Salesforce record, the agent clicks **Submit Moonpull form**.
2. A form tab opens, fills itself in, submits and closes. This takes about 25 seconds, then the agent is back on the record.
3. Moonfill waits for the scan to finish in the background.
4. When the result is ready, Moonfill opens the record in the background, posts the result to its **Chatter** feed and shows a notification.

Each scan moves through clear states: submitting the form, in progress (with a live timer), posting to Salesforce, and complete. A failed scan says why, so the agent knows what to do next.

---

## Built to be safe

- **The result must match the scan.** Before posting, Moonfill checks that the result belongs to the MID that was scanned. If it does not, nothing is posted.
- **A safety net for every failure.** If something goes wrong, a **Force paste** button and a **Copy result** button are one click away, and the Teams message still arrives as usual, so a copy always exists.
- **Invalid records are blocked.** If the detected MID is missing or invalid, the button is disabled to prevent accidental submissions.
- **Minimal footprint.** Moonfill only talks to Microsoft Forms, the Moonpull results folder and Salesforce, using the agent's own logins. The scan list is stored on the agent's computer only.

---

## Technologies Used

- **JavaScript (ES6)** — core logic, state handling and event handling
- **Browser extension APIs (Chrome and Edge)** — background messaging, tabs, notifications, DOM injection and storage
- **HTML & CSS** — a compact popup showing running scans, results and history
- **Salesforce (Chatter)** — record detection and result posting
- **Microsoft Forms and Microsoft 365** — the form being submitted and the shared results folder being read

---

## Challenges and Learnings

Working inside Salesforce's dynamic, shadow-DOM-heavy interface required a deep understanding of asynchronous rendering. Moonfill uses smart polling and conditional retries to wait until the right elements exist before it acts.

Reliability was the real challenge: scans take minutes, browsers get minimized, sessions expire and several scans can overlap. Designing for those cases — keeping every scan's state separate, recovering when the browser is reopened, and waiting politely when the browser is hidden because Salesforce cannot draw its page in the background — mattered more than the happy path.

---

## Outcome

The first version of Moonfill cut Moonpull form submission time by **over 80%**. The current version goes further by removing the waiting, copying and pasting that came after the scan, while keeping data accurate through the MID check.

It has become a trusted internal assistant for the integration team and continues to evolve with user feedback.
